**Category:** General Skills | **Difficulty:** Easy | **Source:** picoCTF 2019

## Challenge
Can you convert the number 42 (base 10) to binary (base 2)?

## Approach
Used the divide-by-2 method — repeatedly divided 42 by 2, recording each 
remainder, then read the remainders bottom-to-top to get the binary value.
